Kale/romaine base + chicken (or steak/salmon if you haven't had it today) + roasted veggies + chickpeas or black beans + light or no grain + dressing on the side (balsamic, lime-cilantro, caesar, or pesto — skip hot honey mustard, spicy cashew, miso sesame ginger)

The S'mores Cookie, honestly
It's a real treat — soft cookie, sweetened, refined flour. Not a health food, but not off-limits either. Best timed after a workout (when you're more carb-tolerant) and paired with something with protein rather than eaten alone on an empty stomach. Fine occasionally, just not a daily habit.
Avoid / go easy on
Iced Coconut/Mocha/S'mores Latte with Sweet Cream Cold Foam, Caramel Craziness Latte, Milky Way Latte, Nutty Irishman Latte, Cold Brew and Cream ("like drinking ice cream" — their words), Pumpkin Spice Latte, Chai Tea Latte (ask for lightly sweetened)

Backups: your data lives only on this phone (nowhere else — not on GitHub, not on a server). Use Export my data occasionally to save a backup file, and Import backup if you ever switch phones, reinstall, or clear Safari's data.
Add to your home screen:
1. Open the Files app, find tracker.html
2. Tap it once — it opens in Quick Look preview
3. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow, top-right)
4. If Safari isn't in that first row, tap "Open in Safari" if shown, or scroll/tap More to find it
5. Once it's open in Safari's address bar, tap the Share icon in Safari itself → Add to Home Screen
