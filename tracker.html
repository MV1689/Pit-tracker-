Sweetgreen Go-Tos

Kale Caesar545 cal · 41g protein · 18g carbs — best ratio on the menu
Chicken Pesto Parm525 cal · 35g protein · 38g carbs — good post-workout carb support
Buffalo Chicken555 cal · 31g protein · 32g carbs — spicy, not boring
Guacamole Greens555 cal · 29g protein · 35g carbs — fresh, punchy
Avoid / go easy on
Chicken Sesame Crunch (21g sugar), Hot Honey items, Miso Glazed Salmon/Omega Glow, Harvest Bowl, and the heavier Protein Plates (double rice/quinoa built in)
Build-your-own formula
Kale/romaine base + chicken (or steak/salmon if you haven't had it today) + roasted veggies + chickpeas or black beans + light or no grain + dressing on the side (balsamic, lime-cilantro, caesar, or pesto — skip hot honey mustard, spicy cashew, miso sesame ginger)
